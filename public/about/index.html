About SecureChat

SecureChat is a free web app that pairs you with a random person for a one-to-one text or video chat. There is no account to create and no chat history to keep. You open the page, confirm you are 18 or older, and start talking.

Why it exists

Most random chat sites ask for little and protect even less. SecureChat tries to do the opposite: it encrypts text messages in your browser, keeps nothing after a chat ends, loads no trackers or third-party scripts, and is honest about what it cannot protect.

What you can do

  • Text chat with formatting, emoji, reactions, replies and photos, all end-to-end encrypted.
  • Video chat directly between browsers over WebRTC, with mute, camera off, screen share and picture-in-picture.
  • Match by shared interests or language, or create a private room and invite a friend.
  • Skip, report, or hide the chat instantly with the panic button (Alt+X).
  • Install it as an app on your phone or computer.

How it works

On this site, matchmaking and call setup run without a server of our own, over public MQTT brokers. Your browser and your partner's agree an encryption key directly (ECDH P-256) and encrypt each text message with AES-256-GCM. Video and audio flow peer to peer, encrypted by WebRTC. The code can also be self-hosted with the included Node.js server, which keeps no database.

Who makes it

SecureChat is an open-source project by 0x0806, published under the MIT licence. It is a hobby-scale project without moderators, so please read the privacy page before relying on it.

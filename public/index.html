Before you start

SecureChat pairs you with strangers. Nobody screens them first, so please read this.

  • People you meet may say or show things you find upsetting or explicit.
  • Text is encrypted end to end. In a video call your partner’s browser connects directly to yours, which lets them see your IP address.
  • Never share your address, passwords, money or anything else you would regret. Do not use SecureChat for anything illegal, or to harass or record people.
  • There are no moderators, and you are responsible for what you do here. You can report anyone.

Rules and privacy

Be decent

Treat people the way you want to be treated. No harassment, hate, sexual content involving minors, threats, spam or illegal activity. Use Report if someone crosses the line.

What the server sees

The server pairs people and relays messages and call setup. It keeps nothing after a chat ends and has no database. It sees your IP address like any web server does, and it writes short operational log lines (such as connection IDs and report reasons).

What is encrypted

Text messages are encrypted in your browser with a key agreed directly with your partner, so the server only relays scrambled text. The key exchange goes through the server, so to be certain nobody is in the middle, compare the safety code in the lock menu with your partner. Video and audio are encrypted by WebRTC and flow directly between browsers.

What you reveal

In a video call your partner’s browser learns your IP address. Use a VPN if that matters to you. Anything you say or show can be recorded by the other person.

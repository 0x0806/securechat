Privacy

Short version: SecureChat has no accounts, no database and no message history. Text messages are encrypted in your browser. Video calls reveal your IP address to the person you are talking to. Last updated 27 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. You are never asked for a name, email address or phone number. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts on this site, and no cookies.

What stays on your device

Your consent to the rules and your settings (theme, nickname, interests, language and similar) are saved in your browser's local storage. The list of people you reported lives in session storage and disappears when you close the tab. You can withdraw consent from "Rules and privacy" in the app, or clear the site's data in your browser at any time.

What is encrypted

Text messages, photos, reactions, replies and read receipts are encrypted in your browser with a key agreed directly with your partner (ECDH P-256, AES-256-GCM), so the relay only carries scrambled data. The key exchange passes through the relay, so compare the safety code in the lock menu with your partner to make sure nobody is in the middle. Video and audio are encrypted by WebRTC (DTLS-SRTP).

What others can see

  • The relay (on this site, the public MQTT brokers broker.hivemq.com and broker.emqx.io; or the Node server when self-hosted) sees your IP address, connection IDs, who is paired with whom while chatting, and call-setup data. These brokers are run by third parties.
  • STUN servers (Google's public stun.l.google.com) see your IP address when a video call is set up, so the two browsers can find a direct route to each other. They see no chat content.
  • Your partner learns your IP address in a video call, because the connection is direct. Use a VPN if that matters to you.
  • Anyone you chat with can record what you say or show. Do not share anything you would not want kept.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes your IP address to deliver the page. See GitHub's own privacy statement for details.

Reports

On this site there is no server to receive reports, so reporting moves you on and stops you being matched with that person again in the session. A self-hosted Node server writes report reasons and connection IDs to its log.

Age

SecureChat is for people aged 18 and over only.
